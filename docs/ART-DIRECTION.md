# Boss-centered environments (Prompt B)

The harbor opening is retained; the following five new environments replace the remaining scenery. Six chapters, six sections per chapter, their objectives and the original regular-enemy roster remain playable. The new story follows Echo's cloning supply chain instead of revisiting the first game's stage route.

| Chapter / environment | Boss and reason for the location | Interactive foreground | Parallax layers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coral Causeway — sunken highway / coral canyon | Iron Convoy transports refrigerated clone pods on the temporarily exposed causeway. | Cadillac chase, rammed pod cargo, toll-fort winch, breakable supply crates and drums. | Distant flooded skyline and bridge towers; separate ruined piers; cracked roadway; near rail fragments and moving tidal glints. |
| Verdant Biodome — overgrown refinery | Verdant Regent, a dinosaur fitted with an armored harness and neural collar, guards Echo's hatchery. This replaces the repeated Fessenden monster. | Egg racks, sonic lures and the boss's nest driver; smash the driver to free the Regent. | Glass dome and jungle mountains; intermediate green pipes/vines; mossy service floor; low roots and drifting spores. |
| Geothermal Forge — underground generator | Foreman Cinder casts the neural collars and growth vats using the volcanic shelf's heat. | Three coolant controls, furnace-feed valve, crate/drum supplies and unfinished vat props. | Magma cavern and turbines; copper supports and pipes; riveted metal deck; low rail fragments and rising embers. |
| Skyhook Radar Fortress — cliff-top command site | Dr. Echo broadcasts orders from the poacher radar array and a new lab below it; her volleys, leaps and reinforcements reflect that control. | Three tunable command channels, hero-copy tanks, breakable stores. | Purple storm cliffs and dishes; independent suspended cables, sweep light and distant pterosaurs; wet fort deck; near broken railing. Pterosaurs are scenery, not invisible combat hazards. |
| Crown Spire — gothic skyscraper over the dam | Marshal Sable commands the clone army from the tower; the final Crown Engine controls the spillway. | Command pylons free allied copies; a final manual release floods the vat turbines after the machine is beaten. | Broken gothic arches and dam skyline; closer stone columns; golden overlook floor; near stone fragments and windborne dust. |

Common camera rates are 0.07 for the distant artwork, 0.32 for intermediate structures, 1.0 for the ground and 1.12 for low foreground silhouettes. Repeating panels alternate horizontal orientation to avoid hard tile seams. Combat stays on the original readable lane band; decorative hazards never inflict unannounced damage. The original heroes, their portraits and regular enemy sprites are untouched. New boss equipment and moving set dressing are drawn in code. The `environment-art.js` manifest loads five local lossless WebP images from `assets/scenery/`. These use ordinary image loading, which also works offline from a local-file launch. Their decoded RGBA pixels were verified identical to the original generated PNGs; no artwork was edited during encoding.
