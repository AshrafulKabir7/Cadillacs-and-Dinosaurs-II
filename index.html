Six months after the lab blew

Nobody owns the water.
Sable wants to.

The flood that swallowed Dr. Fessenden's lab drowned half the east coast. Marshal Sable seized the Crown dam, the only thing holding back the sea, and now every tap runs on his permit.

  1. 01

    Labour for the Crown

    Ration breakers are sedated, sealed in pods and shipped to the dam. Their crime: drawing water without a permit.

  2. 02

    “Order keeps the water flowing.”

    Every ration notice carries Sable's brass gate seal. His supply chain runs the length of the coast, and a boss guards every link.

  3. 03

    Break the chain

    Barge, convoy, hatchery, salvage pit, signal dish, dam. At the top, Sable would rather become the gate than open it. Meet Crownback.

Made like a cabinet

Arcade feel, browser convenience

  • Decoded from the original

    Sprites, moves, frame timings and even how each hero holds every gun were read out of the 1993 arcade data and measured against the cabinet, frame by frame.

  • Coin-op continues

    Out of lives? Insert a coin and get back in right where you fell, before the ten-second countdown runs out.

  • Four-to-five-minute chapters

    Six chapters, each with a mission and a boss with a second form. A full chapter fits in a coffee break.

  • Plays on your phone

    A thumb stick that runs when you push to the edge, big action buttons and landscape fullscreen.

  • No install, no account

    Open the page and play. Checkpoints save in your browser, and the game even runs from a downloaded folder.

  • A sound room

    The soundtrack and effects have their own jukebox on the title screen.

Questions

Before you insert a coin

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account and nothing to buy. It's a fan project.

Is this an official Capcom game?

No. It's an unofficial, non-commercial fan sequel, not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom. The original game, its characters and its art belong to their owners.

Do I need to have played the 1993 game?

No. The story starts six months after the original ending, and the title screen's attract story and the chapter cards catch you up.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. On a touch screen the game shows a thumb stick and action buttons. Landscape gives you the biggest screen.

How does online co-op work?

One player hosts and gets a code like DINO-4KQ7; up to two friends join with the code or the link. The host's game runs the action and the others stream their inputs to it. Everyone needs a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Will my progress save?

Your last checkpoint and your settings are saved in this browser. Choose Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Any standard gamepad works in single player and online.
