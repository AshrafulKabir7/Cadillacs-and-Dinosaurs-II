Same cages. New paint.

Six months after Fessenden’s lab fell, the Black Marketeers who ran his shipments are back, taking dinosaurs alive under the crown of Marshal Sable and his “protective” militia.

  1. They’re taking them alive now. Uniformed guards round up dockworkers in the City in the Sea.
  2. The protection racket. His scouts drive frightened dinosaurs at the settlements; his uniforms arrive to sell the protection.
  3. The Crown Tide. Follow the trail from the swamp camps to the Crown dam and stop the flood Sable means to drive the valley into his pens.

Eight stages · eight bosses

Every stage is three areas, a mission and a boss fight, about four minutes each.

  1. 01
    City in the Sea · Old TroubleWarden Rook: The dock jailer lowers his hook before he pulls. Change lanes when it drops.
  2. 02
    The Swamp Forest · Stolen NestsHeadkeeper Thorne: She throws bait to bring the raptors in. Smash her collar winch.
  3. 03
    The Coral Causeway · Run Them DownHaulmaster Krag: The Cadillac is back. Ram the trailers loose, then meet Krag on foot.
  4. 04
    The Sink · What Was Left BehindForeman Cinder: Watch his gauge: red means a steam shot down your lane.
  5. 05
    Crown Valley · The Protection RacketIronclub: Jump his stomping charge; step aside when he curls into a ball.
  6. 06
    The Verdant Biodome · The Price of PowerGlassback: When her harness splits, everything she has comes at once.
  7. 07
    The Observatory · Echoes of FessendenDr. Echo: She climbs into a walker herself. Change lanes when it glows.
  8. 08
    The Crown Dam · No One Owns the ValleyMarshal Sable: “If you won’t kneel to the marshal, kneel to this!”

Before you insert a coin

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account and nothing to buy. It’s a fan project.

Is this an official Capcom game?

No. It’s an unofficial, non-commercial fan sequel, not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.

Do I need to have played the 1993 game?

No. The story starts six months after the original ending, and the title screen catches you up.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. A touch screen gets a thumb stick and action buttons. Landscape gives the biggest screen.

Will my progress save?

Your last checkpoint and settings are saved in this browser. Choose Continue on the title screen.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Any standard gamepad works in single player and online.
